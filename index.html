Certificate III in Work Health and Safety: Is It Your Next Step?

A plain-English guide for people who carry out safety duties alongside their main job.

What the qualification is

The BSB30719 Certificate III in Work Health and Safety is a nationally recognised AQF Level 3 qualification. It has 11 units (6 core and 5 elective) and is studied online and self-paced, with practical workplace tasks instead of exams.

Who it suits

It may suit you if you already have safety duties on top of your main job, if you are the person your team asks about hazards and procedures, or if you are a fire warden, first aid officer or safety contact for your team.

What to check before you enrol

Check who issues the qualification, compare the unit list on training.gov.au with the work you do, and confirm the entry requirement. You need either a formal qualification or to complete a short language, literacy, numeracy and digital assessment.

Starting at an entry level instead?

3CIR Online also offers one Certificate II, the BSB20120 Certificate II in Workplace Skills, an AQF Level 2 qualification for entry-level business roles.
