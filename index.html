Certificate III in Work Health and Safety: A Practical Overview

Who this nationally recognised safety qualification is designed for, and what to confirm first.

About the course

The BSB30719 Certificate III in Work Health and Safety sits at AQF Level 3 and is nationally recognised. Students complete 11 units, made up of 6 core and 5 elective, entirely online and at their own pace. Assessment is based on practical workplace tasks rather than exams.

Where it fits

This course is a good match for people whose job already includes safety responsibilities, such as the colleague who gets the hazard and procedure questions, or the team fire warden, first aid officer or safety contact.

Questions to settle before enrolling

Confirm which organisation issues the qualification, and compare the units listed on training.gov.au against your own work. Entry needs either a formal qualification or a short language, literacy, numeracy and digital assessment.

If you are just starting out

Anyone beginning at an entry level can look at the Certificate II at 3CIR Online. It is the BSB20120 Certificate II in Workplace Skills, an AQF Level 2 qualification aimed at entry-level business roles.
